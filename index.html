About

The person behind the content.

I'm a social media manager and digital marketing specialist based in Nairobi. I work with brands across healthcare, retail, and services to build presence, grow audiences, and drive measurable results.

My approach is straightforward: understand the brand, create content that resonates, engage with the community, and track what works. I've grown Instagram followings by over 500%, created viral video content for hospital events, and contributed to a 91% increase in online sales.

Before social media, I worked in customer success and field marketing, running brand activations for Blueband, Always, and Nestlé. That ground-level experience shapes how I think about content: it's not just about likes, it's about real people and real business outcomes.
